# DeenFlow Branding Asset

DeenFlow uses a restrained deep-forest launcher icon with a warm ivory “D”, gold rule, and sage accent rule. The artwork is installed in the required Expo build paths: `assets/images/icon.png`, `assets/images/splash-icon.png`, `assets/images/favicon.png`, and `assets/images/android-icon-foreground.png`.

The managed branding reference is configured in `app.config.ts` through its `logoUrl` value, while the local PNGs ensure the Android, splash, and favicon asset slots remain build compatible.
